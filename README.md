# Kompakt

A small macOS menu bar app for optimizing and converting media files.

Kompakt stays out of the way until you need it. Start dragging a supported file and a dropzone appears on the right side of your screen. Drop the file in, choose whether to optimize or convert when both are available, and Kompakt handles the rest.

## How it works

Drag a media file.

Drop it into Kompakt to optimize it, or into the conversion area to change formats.

Get the finished file with a clear size comparison.

That’s the whole flow. No upload page, no file picker, no export screen, no extra window to manage.

## Supported file types

Kompakt can optimize:

- PNG
- JPG / JPEG
- WebP
- GIF
- SVG
- MP4
- MOV
- M4V

Kompakt can convert:

- PNG to JPG or WebP
- JPG / JPEG to PNG or WebP
- WebP to PNG or JPG
- MOV / M4V to MP4

## Settings

Kompakt keeps the controls simple.

You can choose between **Lossless** and **Smaller** (lossy) optimization, decide whether videos should keep their original size or be resized to **1080p** or **720p**, and choose whether optimized files should replace the original or be saved as copies. Converted files always keep the original.

## Menu bar

The menu bar panel shows your recent kompaktions and gives you quick access to settings, updates, and quitting the app.

## Status

Kompakt is early and intentionally simple.

Right now it focuses on quick image and video optimization and conversion through a lightweight macOS menu bar workflow.
